Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data: Name, email address, phone number, billing address, and account preferences.

Fitness & Biometric Data: Body measurements, weight, InBody scan logs, exercise history, macro targets, and progress photos.

Wearable & Health Integrations: Heart rate, step count, calories burned, and sleep duration synced via Apple Health, Garmin, Fitbit, or Google Fit.

2. How We Use Your Information

To deliver personalized fitness programming, coach check-ins, macro recommendations, and habit tracking.

To process payment transactions and manage subscription renewals.

To communicate app updates, challenge announcements, and coach feedback.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to third-party advertisers.

Data is shared only with trusted operational providers necessary to deliver our service (e.g., Trainerize for the coaching platform, payment processors, and analytics partners).

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard encryption, SSL protocols, and access controls to safeguard your data both in transit and at rest.

You may request account deletion or data export at any time by emailing info@team2ndwind.com.
