1. Free Trial Cancellation

Free trials allow full access to T2W Fit On-Demand with no initial charge.

If you cancel before your free period expires, your card will not be billed. If you choose not to cancel, your chosen recurring plan will begin automatically at the conclusion of the trial.

2. Subscription Refunds

Digital Subscriptions: Monthly recurring subscription fees (e.g., On-Demand, Digital Plus, Online Coaching) are non-refundable once billed for the current billing cycle.

When you cancel, you will retain access to the app until the end of your current paid billing period.

3. Transformation Programs, One-Time Programs & Challenges

Program sales (such as 8-Week Transformation Programs, one-time training programs, or special promotional offers) are final upon program activation.

If you experience extenuating medical circumstances, you may request a program credit or pause by submitting medical documentation to info@team2ndwind.com.
