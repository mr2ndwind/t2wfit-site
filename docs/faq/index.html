FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about plans, the free trial, coaching, and how the app works.

What comes with the free trial?

Start with T2W Fit On-Demand free for 14 days. You get the full on-demand workout library, exercise demonstrations, workout tracking, progress tracking, wearable compatibility, and mobile access. After the trial your plan renews at $14.99/month unless you cancel before the trial ends.

Which plan should I choose?

Choose On-Demand if you want the workout library on your own schedule, Digital Plus if you want a structured plan with accountability, Online Coaching if you want a coach to personalize everything remotely, and Hybrid Coaching if you want weekly one-on-one training at T2W Fit Studio plus app coaching. Not sure? Use the plan finder on this page or ask a coach.

What is the difference between Digital Plus and Online Coaching?

Digital Plus gives you the structured system: scheduled training plans, goal-based recommendations, habit tracking, and a monthly progress review. Online Coaching adds a coach who personalizes and adjusts your programming, checks in weekly, and messages you directly.

Do I still receive a personalized training plan?

It depends on the plan. On-Demand gives you the full workout library to follow on your own. Digital Plus adds scheduled training plans and goal-based recommendations. Online Coaching and Hybrid Coaching include personalized programming from a T2W coach. Transformation and one-time programs are structured plans, not fully customized coaching.

Can I cancel anytime?

You can cancel a recurring subscription any time in your app profile settings or by emailing info@team2ndwind.com. To avoid being charged for the next billing cycle, submit your cancellation at least 48 hours before your renewal date. You keep access until the end of your current paid period. Monthly subscription fees are non-refundable once billed. See our Refund & Cancellation policy for details.

Where do I access the workouts?

Everything lives inside the T2W Fit Training App, powered by Trainerize. Download the app, log in with your account, open the Workouts tab, pick a category, and press play.

Can beginners use these workouts?

Yes. The library and programs run from beginner through advanced, with exercise demonstrations, built-in modifications, and lower-impact options so you can start where you are.

Can I use the workouts at home?

Yes. Train at home, in the gym, or while traveling. Our programs are labeled with the equipment they need, and many have home and bodyweight options.

Do I need gym equipment?

No. Many plans work with no equipment or a pair of dumbbells. Each program lists what it needs, from bodyweight only to full gym access.

Can athletes use it?

Yes. The app supports power, speed, and agility work for athletes. Talk to a coach about athletic performance programming that fits your sport and season.

Can I connect my smartwatch?

Compatible devices and health apps can sync with the app, including Apple Health, Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin, Google Fit, Samsung Health, and Withings. Availability depends on your device, operating system, account type, and region.

Does it include nutrition?

Nutrition support grows with your plan. Snap-and-track meal logging is included on On-Demand, Digital Plus, Online Coaching, and Hybrid Coaching. Digital Plus adds nutrition resources, and Online Coaching and Hybrid Coaching include nutrition and habit coaching. Nutrition content is general education, not medical nutrition therapy.

How do I contact my coach?

Online Coaching and Hybrid Coaching include direct in-app coach messaging and weekly check-ins. Digital Plus includes in-app support. For any plan, you can also email info@team2ndwind.com or call or text 918-918-3234.

Are Les Mills workouts included with every membership?

Les Mills-inspired programming is available through the T2W Fit Training App with qualifying memberships. Availability may depend on the app or coaching plan you choose, so ask a coach which library options come with your plan.

How do one-time programs work?

One-time programs are 4-week ($29) or 8-week ($50) training plans you buy once. There is no subscription and no renewal. After checkout your program is delivered inside the T2W Fit Training App.

Is the Transformation Program personalized one-on-one coaching?

No. Transformation Programs are structured 8-week experiences with workouts, meal plans, habit tracking, and in-app accountability. For personalized coaching, choose Online Coaching or Hybrid Coaching.

Do I need a gym membership for the Transformation Programs?

No. Both levels include home and gym workout options.

Does a T2W Fit Studio membership include the app?

T2W Studio Unlimited Plus includes the T2W Fit Digital Plus app at no extra cost. For other studio memberships, ask a coach about digital options. See the studio site for membership details.
