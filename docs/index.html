Frequently Asked Questions

What comes with the free trial?

Start with T2W Fit On-Demand free for 14 days. You get the full on-demand workout library, exercise demonstrations, workout tracking, progress tracking, wearable compatibility, and mobile access. After the trial your plan renews at $14.99/month unless you cancel before the trial ends.

Where do I access the workouts?

Everything lives inside the T2W Fit Training App, powered by Trainerize. Download the app, log in with your account, open the Workouts tab, pick a category, and press play.

Can beginners use these workouts?

Yes. The library and programs run from beginner through advanced, with exercise demonstrations, built-in modifications, and lower-impact options so you can start where you are.

Do I still receive a personalized training plan?

It depends on the plan. On-Demand gives you the full workout library to follow on your own. Digital Plus adds scheduled training plans and goal-based recommendations. Online Coaching and Hybrid Coaching include personalized programming from a T2W coach. Transformation and one-time programs are structured plans, not fully customized coaching.

Can I cancel anytime?

You can cancel a recurring subscription any time in your app profile settings or by emailing info@team2ndwind.com. To avoid being charged for the next billing cycle, submit your cancellation at least 48 hours before your renewal date. You keep access until the end of your current paid period. Monthly subscription fees are non-refundable once billed. See our Refund & Cancellation policy for details.

Do I need gym equipment?

No. Many plans work with no equipment or a pair of dumbbells. Each program lists what it needs, from bodyweight only to full gym access.
